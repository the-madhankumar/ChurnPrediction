### 89. Simple explanation

**Rule-based scoring:**

1. I define simple conditions, such as month-to-month contract, low tenure, and high monthly charges.
2. If a customer meets all the conditions, I flag them as high-risk.
3. It is easy to understand and explain.
4. But it may miss other important patterns.

**ML-based scoring:**

1. ML looks at many customer features together.
2. It learns patterns from previous customer churn data.
3. It can give a risk score or probability.
4. This can find patterns that simple rules may not catch.

**In simple words:**

> “The rule-based approach is easy to understand and act on quickly, while ML can find more complex patterns and make more detailed predictions.”
